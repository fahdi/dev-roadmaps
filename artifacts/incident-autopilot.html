Pipeline Failure Autopilot

A scheduled job fails at 02:00. Does a human have to wake up? Agents can gather the evidence and form a hypothesis in seconds, but whether anything is allowed to act on it is a different question, and it is not the router that answers it.

Idempotent?

Retry only if safe

A retry is a write. Rerunning a job that charges a card or appends to a ledger turns one failure into two problems. Transience is a necessary condition, never a sufficient one.

Resume the failed task

Rerun the task that broke, not the whole graph. Re-running upstream work that already succeeded is how an automated recovery quietly doubles a day's rows.

Close only after verification

A green retry is not a resolved incident until the output is checked. Anything that can close its own ticket needs to prove the result, not just the exit code.

The agents are the cheap part. The gate is the product.
Diagnosing a failure automatically is now easy. Deciding that a machine may act on that diagnosis, unattended, at two in the morning, is an engineering commitment about idempotency and blast radius.
Build the gate first, and the autopilot is a feature. Build it last, and it is an outage generator.
Roadmap: route, gather evidence, then stop at a gate you designed on purpose. Escalation is a correct outcome, not a failure of the system.
