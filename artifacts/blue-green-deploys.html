Blue-Green Deploys

Two identical environments, one router, and a switch that is genuinely instant. The diagram is easy. What it leaves out is the database they both share, which is the thing that decides whether your rollback is real or imaginary.

Flipping the router back takes a second. Un-dropping a column does not.
Blue-green gives you an instant rollback for your code, and nothing at all for your data.
Ship the schema change and the code that needs it as two separate deploys, and the rollback stays as fast as the diagram promises.
Roadmap: expand the schema, deploy the code, contract later. Never in one release.
