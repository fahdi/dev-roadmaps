Developer Roadmap

Fine-Tune an LLM, End to End

Fine-tuning does not teach your model new facts. It teaches new behaviour. Examples set the behaviour, loss updates the weights, and gates decide whether any of it ships.

1

Curated Training Examples

Quality decides the ceiling. Everything downstream just amplifies what you put here.

Conversations Support and expert exchanges in the shape you actually want back
Behaviour Style and tool use, demonstrated rather than described
Safety Refusals and edge cases, so the model learns where the walls are
2

Data and Training Engine

Turn raw examples into batches the trainer can consume

Clean→ Template→ Tokenize→ Split

Only the train split reaches the optimizer. Validation and test exist to gate the release, never to train on. The moment a gate set leaks into training, your scores stop meaning anything.

3

Choose What to Update

The single decision that sets your cost, your risk and your rollback story

Full Fine-Tune Update every weight. Maximum control, and a whole new model to store, serve and roll back. Update all weights
LoRA / QLoRA Freeze the base, train small adapters beside it. Cheaper, quicker to iterate, and the base stays reusable. Freeze base, train adapters
4

Supervised Fine-Tuning Loop

The part that actually moves the weights, repeated every batch

Train batch→ Frozen base + adapters→ Forward pass→ Token loss
↵ Backprop→ Optimizer step → Next epoch

Loss trending down is a sign that training is working, not a sign that the model is good. Falling loss on the train split with flat quality on the gate sets means you are memorising, not teaching.

5

Evaluate Before Deploy

Three checks, run on the splits the optimizer never saw

Quality Does it do the task, in the format you asked for
Safety Check for regressions, since new behaviour can quietly undo old refusals
Registry Base plus adapter recorded together, so a result maps to an exact artifact

Passes the gates?

This is a fork, not a formality. A failed gate sends you back to the data, because that is nearly always where the problem started.

No → fix the data and retrain Yes → ship it carefully
6

Version, Canary, Monitor

Shipping a model is a rollout, not a file copy

Version base + adapter→ Canary on small traffic→ Monitor with rollback ready

Pick the abort metric before the canary starts. Deciding what "worse" means while traffic is already flowing is how a bad model stays live.

Teach behaviour. Keep facts fresh.
Fine-tune the things that are stable: tone, format, tool use, refusals.
Retrieve the things that change: prices, policies, documents, anything with a date on it.
A model fine-tuned on facts is out of date the moment those facts move.
Roadmap: curate examples, build the training data, choose full or adapter, run the loop, gate on unseen splits, then version, canary and monitor.
