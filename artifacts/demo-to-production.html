Developer Roadmap

Demo to Production

The same seven capabilities, built two ways. The demo version works on your machine and in a screenshot. The production version survives real users, and the difference is almost never the model.

Every one of these ships as a demo. That is the trap.
The demo column is not wrong, it is unfinished, and it looks identical in a screenshot.
The work that separates the two is unglamorous: measuring retrieval instead of assuming it, bounding an agent instead of trusting it, and being able to prove a change helped instead of feeling that it did.
Roadmap: measure retrieval, bound the agent, curate the context, gate on evals, plan for failure, budget the cost, and treat every input as hostile.
Pairs with How to Build a RAG, 6 Levels of Agentic AI, How to Evaluate an LLM App and Context Engineering.
