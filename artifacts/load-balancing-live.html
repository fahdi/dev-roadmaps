Load Balancing, Watched Live

Four servers, one of them slower than the rest. Every algorithm below spreads the same traffic differently, and the difference only shows up when the backends are not identical. Pick one and watch the queues.

Round Robin counts turns, ignores load

Every algorithm is a guess about what the servers are doing.
Round robin guesses they are identical. Weighted guesses you know the ratio in advance. Least connections and power of two stop guessing and look.
When every backend really is identical and healthy, they all behave the same. The choice only starts to matter on the day one of them is slow, which is the day you need it to.
Roadmap: rotate blindly, weight by known capacity, read live depth, sample two, or pin by key. Add health checks to all of them.
