RAG vs CAG

Retrieval fetches the relevant part of your corpus at question time. Cache-augmented generation skips that by putting the whole corpus in the model's context once and reusing the prefilled cache. Which is available to you is decided almost entirely by one number.

Model context window
How often does the corpus change?

RAG

Retrieve, then generate

CAG

Preload the cache, then generate

CAG removes the retrieval step, and every bug that lives in it.
No chunking, no embedding drift, no top-k that quietly missed the paragraph that mattered. That is a real gain, available only while the whole corpus fits.
The moment it does not, you are doing retrieval again, so build knowing which side of that line you are on.
Roadmap: measure the corpus in tokens first. That single number decides whether the choice is even yours to make.
